Conclusions

  1. The mechanism is correct — raw GPU-memory snapshot/restore of live vLLM LoRA slots and live trainer adapter+optimizer state is bit-stable, including optimizer steps after restore.
  2. The value is the VRAM curve, not per-op latency — snapshot mode holds VRAM constant (~2.1 GB back per parked tenant) while baseline grows linearly with tenants; at 2 tenants baseline wins wall-clock, and the crossover arrives with tenant count and model scale.
  3. The dominant overhead is GPU-CR's 2MB block granularity — ~288MB moved for ~6MB of sampler weights, ~2GB for ~330MB of trainer state; upstream sub-block dumps would cut data moved ~60×.
